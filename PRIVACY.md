# Privacy Policy for Aspect Ratio Changer for YouTube™

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Aspect Ratio Changer for YouTube™ ("the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines our data handling practices.

## 1. No Personal Data Collection
Aspect Ratio Changer for YouTube™ does **not** collect, store, track, or transmit any personally identifiable information, browsing history, account credentials, or user activity.

## 2. Local Operation Only
All operations, including UI injection into the YouTube player and video element transformations, run 100% locally on your browser. No data is transmitted off your device or sent to any external servers, third-party analytics, or telemetry providers.

## 3. Permissions Used
The extension requests content script access to `https://www.youtube.com/*` and `https://youtube.com/*` exclusively to:
- Render the aspect ratio selector button inside the YouTube player control bar.
- Apply CSS scale and position adjustments to video playback elements when selected by the user.

No other browser permissions (such as `cookies`, `tabs`, `storage`, or `<all_urls>`) are requested or used.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The extension does not use third-party tracking, advertising, analytics, or remote API services.
